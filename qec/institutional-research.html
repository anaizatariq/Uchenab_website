Institutional research at the University of Chenab supports evidence-based decision making across academic and administrative functions. The QEC collects, validates and analyses institutional data, and reports findings to the University’s statutory bodies so that policies and academic practices are continuously improved.

QEC develops procedures for the following

  • Annual monitoring and evaluation including program monitoring, faculty monitoring, and student perceptions
  • Departmental Review
  • Self-Assessment Reports
  • Quality Assurance through Post Graduate Program Review of MS, MPhil, and PhD degree programs
  • Institutional assessment
  • Continuous Quality Improvement (CQI)
  • Implementation of all HEC Surveys

A modern, research-driven university committed to producing highly skilled professionals with character, courage and competence.
