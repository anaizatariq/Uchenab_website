Program Overview

Bachelor of Sciences in Nursing

Department of Allied Health Sciences, first in the region offers Bachelor of Nursing to students who are taking interest in health & medical care. Our four-year BScN program blends dense academic preparation. It brings innovative teaching methods as well as hands-on training with clinical experiences. This will hence enable you to provide holistic care for individuals. On the successful completion of the BSN completion, the graduates hence get an opportunity to enter the nursing profession.

Modules

The BS Nursing curriculum is aiming to polish the students so that they can pursue nursing as a professional career. The students have to study various subjects that hence refer to the roles of the nurse. The conceptual basis for nursing practice is hence the basic need in the health care departments. The compulsory subjects include English, Pakistan Studies, Islamic Studies / Ethics, Mathematics as well as Introduction to Computer. There are different elective subjects and some of them are Microbiology, Anatomy& Physiology, Biochemistry, Ethics, Pathophysiology, Nutrition as well as Psychology.  

Eligibility Criteria

12 years of education/HSSC/A-Level/Intermediate (Pre-Medical) or equivalent with minimum 50% marks. UCHENAB Entry Test is mandatory.

Teaching

In teaching, objective and practical form of methodology is part of this degree. Highly skillful teachers are driven to deliver research-based knowledge. State of the art lecture rooms alongside labs are available with modern tools of learning. Meanwhile, arrangement of lectures is on the clinical perspectives. Different teaching methodologies including assignments, presentations, quizzes, seminars, group discussions as well as activities are part of it. This program consists of 137 credit hours with 44 core courses as well as one elective course. After completion of Four-Years BS Nursing students will be able to:

  • Applying the nursing process to provide safe, competent, and holistic nursing care to patients/clients in variety of settings.
  • Utilizing effective critical thinking as well as problem-solving in their professional practice.
  • Using scholarly skills and the use of scientific methods in critiquing and applying existing nursing theories and research findings in selected practice settings.
  • Utilizing effective oral and written communication in interpersonal as well as professional interactions with peers, clients, families, and other professionals.
  • Developing leadership skills to hence promote the development of the profession.

Clinical Practice

The University of Chenab offers supervised clinical rotations through its affiliated teaching hospitals, providing students with hands-on clinical training, practical experience, and professional exposure in real healthcare settings.

Affiliated Hospitals for Clinical Rotations:

PESSI Hospital, Gujranwala
PESSI Hospital, Gujrat
Gujrat Hospital
Punjab Medical College (PMC) Hospital, Sialkot*
Other affiliated teaching hospitals and healthcare institutions, as approved by the Faculty of Allied Health Sciences.

Assessment

The division of the marks will be according to the subject, include theory and practical performance. The pass percentage will be 50%. The students will have to pass the theory and practical examinations separately.

Careers

Graduate of Bachelor of Nursing (BSN), can work in a variety of practices such as hospitals, long-term care facilities, community and public health agencies, ambulatory care centers, public health service, military services, administration medical centers and in any setting where health care services are necessary. It includes operation room, critical care and emergency departments as well. The nurse seeks the specialty after many years of practice. More possibilities in the nursing profession, such as leadership, teaching, or management, are frequently a result.
