Program Overview

Mathematics (BS)

The Department of Physical Sciences is one of the primary departments of The University of Chenab. The Department realizes its responsibility to ensure the long-term and result-oriented exuberance of Mathematical Sciences, that it should foster the development of mathematicians as future scholars, teachers, researchers, and organizational leaders.

The department of Physical Sciences offers you the degree that will gear your strong mathematical ability and commitment enabling the courage to explore the utmost passion for mathematics. The Bachelor of Science Honors in Mathematics is an undergraduate specialization degree that aims at integrating and deepening the knowledge and prowess in mathematics. It further develops students’ capacity to conduct supervised research.

The Bachelor of Science Honors in Mathematics develops

The Bachelor of Science Honors in Mathematics develops and articulates a real process to evaluate and understand the numerical and graphical information in the course and society at large. The expertise of the instructors always immaculate applications of mathematics in problem-solving, finance, probability, statistics, geometry, population growth. The Department of Mathematics offers programs leading to Bachelor of Science degrees in pure mathematics and applied mathematics.

A degree in mathematics provides an outstanding base on which to build a career in virtually any high technology field and provides a particularly absolute foundation for advanced study in engineering, finance, and science.

As a mathematics major, you will be thoroughly trained in classical mathematics, in addition to being exposed to ideas on the cutting edge of current research. Your training will include the use of the latest technological tools, and you will have access to the department’s computer labs, equipped with workstations and the latest mathematical software.

Modules

The Bachelor of Science Honors in Mathematics is a four-year program designed for students wishing to attain a thorough grounding for pure Mathematics at an advanced level, as an autonomous qualification.

Our degree program consists of core and optional modules. In core modules, you will study essential topics in algebra, analysis, and applied mathematics. Optional modules cover the entire range of mathematical sciences, including algebra, combinatorics, number theory, geometry, topology, pure and applied analysis, differential equations, and applications to physical, biological, and data sciences.

There are core modules in the first and second years of study. The third-year comprises optional modules. In the fourth year, the research activities are planned in modern areas of Mathematics to find solutions to current mathematical problems.

Eligibility Criteria

For Students with an ADP Degree:

ADP in Other Disciplines:
Must have studied Mathematics in FA/FSc or equivalent.
Minimum 60% marks in Mathematics in FA/FSc or equivalent.
Required to complete a bridge semester to address deficiencies.
Total number of credit hours # 81.
For Students with BA/BSc or Equivalent Degree:
Must have completed a BA/BSc or equivalent 14-year education.
Minimum 45% marks in BA/BSc.
Must have studied Mathematics in FA/FSc or equivalent.
Minimum 60% marks in Mathematics in FA/FSc Mathematics or equivalent.
Required to complete a bridge semester to address deficiencies.
Total number of credit hours # 81.

Teaching

The Department of Mathematics focuses on the scientific and logical learning environment to provide the students with a zestful learning experience. Auxiliary online materials are available and there will be various events, research-based national and international seminars, and activities to further enhance the learning and logical reasoning. The chief purpose of The Department of mathematics is to provide a skilled and result-oriented ambiance. As a research student, your closest contact will be with your supervisor and the project instructor who will guide you.

In the fourth year, you meet your supervisor (a graduate student or final-year undergraduate) twice a week to discuss the course material and go over submitted work. In your second and third years, lecture modules are accompanied by weekly support classes. Your tutor provides a further layer of learning and pastoral sup.

Assessment

The University’s policy on assessment for coursework programs is based on the following four principles:

  1. Assessment. Must encourage and. reinforce learning.
  2. Assessment. Must enable robust and fair. Judgments about student performance.
  3. Assessment. Practices must be fair and equitable to students and give them the opportunity to demonstrate what they have learned.
  4. Assessment. Must maintain academic standards.

Careers

The Department of Mathematics is established to train the students to become competent professionals and researchers by earning an M.Phil. Degree in Mathematics. There are numerous opportunities for mathematics majors to undertake research projects under the supervision of immaculate faculty members; like Actuaries, economists and statisticians, Software developers, Chartered and certified accountants, Finance and investment analysts, Teachers, Telecommunication designers, Data scientists and engineers, Academics.
