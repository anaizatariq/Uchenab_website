Program Overview

The Higher National Diploma (HND) in Art and Design at the University of Chenab offers students an immersive educational experience through applied learning. This program provides a comprehensive study of the Art and Design sector, making it ideal for those who wish to pursue or advance their careers in this creative field.

Students gain essential knowledge, understanding, and practical skills foundational to the industry through our HND in Art and Design. Additionally, the program offers hands-on experience, exposing students to the full breadth and depth of the Art and Design sector. This robust preparation equips graduates for further academic pursuits or specialized training in various art and design careers.

  • Internationally Recognized Qualification: Offered by Pearson Edexcel, London, UK.
  • Global Reach: Pearson Edexcel operates in 112 countries, with over four million students annually pursuing its internationally recognized qualifications.
  • Equivalent to Two-Year BA/BSc in Pakistan: Providing a solid foundation for further academic pursuits.
  • Affordable and Economical: Accessible education without compromising on quality.
  • Direct Employment Opportunities: In-depth professional education highly regarded by employers.
  • Pathway to Further Education: Successful completion of HND allows direct entry to a one-year top-up to a BA/BSc Honors Degree at UK universities.
  • Early Professional Training: Ideal for students looking to start their professional journey early.

Assignment-Based Assessment: UCI supports students in completing their HND on time.

Careers

At the University of Chenab, our Fashion Design program is designed to equip you with the employability skills essential for thriving in the creative industries, particularly in Fashion Design. We prepare you for the real-world context of the industry, helping you develop a clear strategy for securing the right employment upon graduation.

Professional Practice and Career Management

Professional practice is a core focus throughout your studies. Our work-related learning unit will sharpen your ability to manage your career effectively. You’ll receive guidance on creating a professional portfolio, crafting a standout CV, and mastering social media skills to promote yourself in today’s digital art and design landscape.

Comprehensive Skill Development

By studying a broad range of topics, you’ll have the flexibility to choose your career path upon graduation. Throughout the program, you will:

  • Master specialized design and craft skills crucial for success in the domestic and international fashion industry.
  • Study in a vibrant, dedicated studio environment, complete with individual sewing machines for every student.
  • Develop your unique creative approach to designing and manufacturing a diverse range of garments.
  • Gain expertise in digital photography, pattern cutting, computer-aided design (CAD), digital textile printing, and high-quality screen printing.
  • Explore multicultural and international contexts to enrich your design ideas and creative processes.
  • Challenge established designers by preparing a garment collection and showcasing your work at our Graduate Fashion Show.
  • Acquire design and manufacturing skills that are highly transferable within the fashion industry.

Career Opportunities

Graduates from our Fashion Design program are well-prepared to pursue a variety of careers in the fashion industry, including:

  • Contemporary Fashion and Textile Design: Fashion Designers, Fashion Stylists, Textile Developers, Pattern Cutters
  • Related Design Practice Areas: Teaching, CAD/CAM Sample Makers, Technologists

With the skills and experience gained at the University of Chenab, you’ll be ready to make your mark in the fashion world. Explore the exciting career opportunities that await you in Fashion Design!
