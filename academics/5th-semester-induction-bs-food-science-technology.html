Program Overview

Food Science & Technology (BS)

The BS Food Science & Technology 5th Semester Induction program at The University of Chenab is designed for students who have completed a two-year Associate Degree Program and wish to convert it into a full four-year bachelor degree. Students join the program directly in the fifth semester and complete the remaining two years of study.

Food Science & Technology brings chemistry, microbiology, engineering and nutrition together to answer a single question: how do we produce food that is safe, nutritious, affordable and sustainable? Students study the composition of foods, how they change during processing and storage, and how quality and safety are engineered into a product from raw material to retail shelf.

Teaching combines laboratory work, product development projects and industrial exposure so that graduates leave with the practical competence expected by food manufacturers, quality assurance departments and regulatory bodies in Pakistan and abroad.

Modules

The remaining two years of the degree are delivered over four semesters of core and elective study, followed by a supervised final-year project.

  • Food Chemistry
  • Food Microbiology
  • Food Processing and Preservation
  • Food Analysis and Instrumentation
  • Food Quality Management and Safety Systems (HACCP, ISO 22000)
  • Dairy Technology
  • Cereal, Fruit and Vegetable Technology
  • Meat and Poultry Technology
  • Food Packaging
  • Food Engineering and Unit Operations
  • Food Product Development
  • Food Laws and Regulations
  • Research Methodology
  • Final Year Research Project / Internship

Course titles and credit distribution follow the approved HEC undergraduate framework and are confirmed by the Department at the start of each academic session.

Eligibility Criteria

For Students with an ADP Degree: ADP in Food Science & Technology or a closely related discipline gives direct admission to the 5th semester.

Minimum CGPA: 2.0/4.0.

Total number of credit hours # 66.

ADP in Other Disciplines: Must have studied Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering or an equivalent science group at FSc level.

Minimum 60% marks in the relevant science subjects at FSc or equivalent.

Required to complete a bridge semester to address deficiencies.

For Students with BA/BSc or Equivalent Degree: Must have completed a BSc or equivalent 14-year education with a science background.

Minimum 45% marks in BSc.

Required to complete a bridge semester to address deficiencies.

Tuition Fee

Tuition fees cover the majority of the costs of your study, including teaching and assessment. Fees are charged at the start of each academic semester. If you pay your fees directly to the University, you can also choose to pay in instalments.

Additional course costs

There may be extra costs related to your course for things such as stationery, books, laboratory consumables, materials and field trips.

The fee schedule for BS Food Science & Technology (5th Semester Induction) is issued by the Treasurer’s Office before each intake. For the current per-credit-hour rate and semester-wise breakdown, contact the Admissions Office at info@uchenab.edu.pk or 053 111 243 622.

Funding

We offer several fully and partially funded undergraduate scholarships to full-time undergraduate students. Visit Funding Policy.

Teaching

Teaching is delivered through lectures, tutorials, laboratory sessions and supervised project work. Departmental laboratories support food analysis, microbiology and product development, and students undertake industrial visits and an internship with a food processing or quality assurance organisation.

Assessment

Assessment combines sessional work, mid-term and final examinations, laboratory reports, product development portfolios, presentations and a final-year research project or internship report.

Careers

Graduates work across the food value chain in production, quality and research roles.

  • Quality Assurance / Quality Control Officer
  • Production and Process Supervisor
  • Product Development Technologist
  • Food Safety and Compliance Officer
  • Food Analyst in public and private laboratories
  • Nutrition and Food Regulatory Advisor
  • Entrepreneurship in food processing and value addition
  • Postgraduate study, including the MPhil Food Science and Technology at UChenab

Departments

The program is delivered by the Department of Food Science & Technology within the Faculty of Allied Health Sciences at The University of Chenab.

For program advice, contact the Department through the Admissions Office at info@uchenab.edu.pk.
