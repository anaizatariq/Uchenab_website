Program Overview

Doctor of Physio Therapy DPT Program Overview

The Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) program at the University of Chenab is specifically for students who aspire to excel in the field of Physical Therapy. Graduates of this program will earn the title of “Physiotherapist”. They will be hence standard with the prefix “Dr” and the suffix “PT.” This degree provides students with the professional skills necessary to secure rewarding positions as physiotherapists in various healthcare settings.

The University of Chenab offers a comprehensive five-year Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) program. It is hence available to provide in-depth knowledge of the modern healthcare system and clinical practice. This program focuses on patient-centred rehabilitation counseling as well as advanced treatment skills.

As a branch of medicine and health sciences, the DPT program emphasizes the science of healing and the art of compassionate care. The curriculum is available to deliver high-quality clinical education, preparing graduates to rehabilitate patients with different functional disorders. These include musculoskeletal, physiological, structural, neurological, cardiovascular, as well as psychosomatic conditions. Our graduates take care of the elderly, handicapped, and those with severe functional impairments.

On-Campus Learning:

Our on-campus program integrates theoretical knowledge with practical application through various therapeutic methods and modalities. Students will learn to treat injuries related to muscles, tendons, joints, and bones, which may result from accidents, trauma, surgery, fractures, or congenital and acquired diseases.

Professional Expertise:

As a student of the DPT program, you will gain proficiency in the latest scientific and clinical techniques. You will be hence visiting different hospitals to alleviate pain and other complications. The program covers a wide range of treatment methods. Few common are diagnosis, evaluation, examination, use of physical agents, movement therapies, as well as manual therapy.

By choosing the Doctor of Physical Therapy program at The University of Chenab, you will benefit from a curriculum that blends rigorous academic instruction with hands-on clinical experience. Our goal is to develop expert physiotherapists who are capable of providing high-quality care. They should be hence contributing in improving patient outcomes in various healthcare settings.

Modules

The Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) program at the University of Chenab follows the guidelines set by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan. The curriculum is designed to provide comprehensive education and training in physical therapy, ensuring graduates are well-equipped to meet the demands of the profession. Below is a detailed outline of the five-year program modules.

Year 1: Foundations of Physical Therapy

  • Anatomy-I
  • Physiology-I
  • Kinesiology-I
  • Biochemistry
  • Physical Therapy Ethics and Professionalism
  • Introduction to Computers and Information Technology
  • English-I (Functional English)
  • Pakistan Studies

Year 2: Core Physical Therapy Principles

  • Anatomy-II
  • Physiology-II
  • Kinesiology-II
  • Exercise Physiology
  • Biomechanics
  • Pharmacology
  • Medical Physics
  • English-II (Communication Skills)
  • Islamic Studies/Ethics

Year 3: Specialized Physical Therapy Techniques

  • Pathology and Microbiology
  • Clinical Orthopedics
  • Clinical Neurology
  • Musculoskeletal Physical Therapy-I
  • Electrotherapy-I
  • Manual Therapy-I
  • Community Medicine and Rehabilitation
  • English-III (Technical Writing and Presentation Skills)

Year 4: Advanced Clinical Skills

  • Musculoskeletal Physical Therapy-II
  • Neurological Physical Therapy
  • Cardiopulmonary Physical Therapy
  • Pediatrics Physical Therapy
  • Geriatrics Physical Therapy
  • Electrotherapy-II
  • Manual Therapy-II
  • Research Methodology and Evidence-Based Practice

Year 5: Clinical Practice and Research

  • Clinical Practice (Internships and Rotations in Various Departments)
  • Advanced Orthopedic Physical Therapy
  • Advanced Neurological Physical Therapy
  • Women’s Health Physical Therapy
  • Sports Physical Therapy
  • Prosthetics and Orthotics
  • Thesis/Research Project

Program Highlights

– Clinical Internships: Gain hands-on experience in various healthcare settings.

– Research Project: Conduct and present research, contributing to the field of physical therapy.

– Interdisciplinary Learning: Collaborate with professionals from other healthcare disciplines.

– Professional Development: Develop leadership and advocacy skills to become a change-maker in healthcare.

Eligibility Criteria

As per approved curriculum of DPT from HEC, minimum of 60% marks in F.Sc Pre-Medical is required for admission in DPT. But unfortunately, no such documented criteria is found for MS/MPHIL in Physical Therapy to date.

Tuition Fee

Tuition fees cover the majority of the costs of your study, including teaching and assessment. Fees are charged at the start of each academic semester. If you pay your fees directly to the University, you can also choose to pay in instalments.

Additional Courses Cost

There may be extra costs related to your course for things such as stationery, books, materials, and field trips.

Teaching

Throughout the Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) program at the University of Chenab, you will hence engage in a diverse range of educational activities. These include:

  • Lectures and Lab Sessions: Comprehensive lectures as well as hands-on lab sessions to build foundational knowledge and practical skills.
  • Clinical Practice:Real-world experience in clinical settings, hospitals, pediatric institutes, as well as old homes.
  • Research Work:Opportunities to undertake research projects, enhancing your analytical as well as investigative skills.
  • Seminars and Workshops:Interactive sessions to hence deepen your understanding of key topics.
  • Online Learning and Independent Study:Flexible learning options to complement your studies.

Program Structure

The program includes 30-32 lectures with around 2-3 hours of teaching per week. You will be hence guided on how to effectively prepare for classes, read primary texts, and complete research projects.

Clinical Practice

The University of Chenab offers supervised clinical rotations through its affiliated teaching hospitals, providing students with hands-on clinical training, practical experience, and professional exposure in real healthcare settings.

Affiliated Hospitals for Clinical Rotations:

PESSI Hospital, Gujranwala
PESSI Hospital, Gujrat
Gujrat Hospital
Punjab Medical College (PMC) Hospital, Sialkot*
Other affiliated teaching hospitals and healthcare institutions, as approved by the Faculty of Allied Health Sciences.

Assessment

You will be evaluated by a combination of theory and practical work including clinical practice, lab sessions, individual assignments, quizzes, research, and group work presentations.

Careers

Upon completing a Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) program at our university, you can pursue diverse as well as fulfilling career paths, including:

  • Clinical Physical Therapist in hospitals, rehabilitation centers, as well as private clinics
  • Orthopedic Physiotherapist specializing in musculoskeletal disorders
  • Respiratory Therapist aiding patients with respiratory conditions
  • Geriatric Physiotherapist focusing on elderly care
  • Pediatric Physiotherapist assisting children with developmental needs
  • Sports Physiotherapist working with athletes to enhance performance as well as recovery

Additionally, physical therapists can explore roles in research, contributing to advancements in healthcare as well as rehabilitation practices. They may also pursue careers in academia, teaching future generations of healthcare professionals.

Helping you to find a right career:

Our program equips you with hands-on clinical experience and guidance from experienced mentors. Career counsellors are hence available to support your professional development, ensuring you’re prepared for success in the global field of physical therapy. Explore the opportunities our DPT program offers as well as embark on a rewarding career dedicated to improving lives worldwide.
