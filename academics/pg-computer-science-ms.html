Program Overview

Master of Science in Computer Science at the University of Chenab is an advanced-level graduate research degree program for technically minded students who aim to conduct research-related activities in computer science fundamentals. Our Master’s degree is a full-time, two years program that provides a comprehensive knowledge of coursework as well as a research component.

Main Goal

It aims at strengthening the understanding of students for constructing computer programs. This is including key technologies and skills for strong theoretical foundations as well as the practical ability to develop systems and products. This course is particularly available to give you an insight into different areas of computer science. Hence, this includes computer security, machine learning, internet protocols as well as networks. It also counts with artificial intelligence, advanced algorithms, Scientific computing, software technology, data science, theoretical computer science as well as research methodology.

Career Opportunities

While studying with us, you can pursue careers at the frontline of modern software technologies. You can also work in leading software houses, commercial sectors, or working at advanced technology companies. With specialization, you will be ready for a career in research or modern studies towards a doctoral degree. This degree hence helps you to understand and grasp deep knowledge of user experience and computer interactions. You can learn about information security management, data mining, and web-based information system developments.

Eligibility Criteria

Entry Requirements

Before applying for a degree, you should first check that we recognize your undergraduate education qualification. Degree in relevant subject, earned from a recognized university after 16 years of education with at least 60 % marks or CGPA of at least 2.0 on scale of 4.0

The following core courses are suitable to be finish before entering the MSCS program.

  1. Analysis of Algorithms
  2. Assembly Language / Computer Architecture
  3. Computer Networks
  4. Computer Programming
  5. Data Structures
  6. Database Systems
  7. Operating Systems
  8. Software Engineering
  9. Theory of Automata

Hence, a student shortlist for admission having deficiency in the above courses may be necessary to study a maximum of FOUR courses. They should be passing in the first two semester. Deficiency courses shall be determine away by the Graduate Student Committee, before admitting the students.

A student cannot register in MS courses, unless all specified deficiency courses have been passed.

Tuition Fee

Mainly tuition fees majorly covers our study cost, which includes assessment and teaching. Advanced fees are taken before the academic semester. If the university takes fees directly then you can choose the installment methods to pay your fees.

Additional course costs
You may be charged an extra cost of additional things such as books, stationery, recreational and study trips, and other materials.

Funding
We offer several fully and partially funded postgraduate scholarships to full-time postgraduate students. Many external organizations aid in this funding programs which increases the amount of scholarships. For more information about funding visit office of student affairs.

Teaching

In MSCS we use various methods of teachings through classroom activities. This includes with a combination of lectures, tutorials, lab sessions, seminars, workshops, online learning, as well as independent study.

This program structure has a total number of fifteen to sixteen lectures. It has about 3 hours per week will be part for teaching. You get guidance about the studies. This is mainly about time for preparing classes, completion of research projects, as well as reading of primary texts. Hence, this program is a suitable mix of positive learning environment for quality composing work to take place.

Assessment

Mainly evaluation will be concluded by extensive monitoring of written examination and course work completion along with routine quizzes, individual assignments, and group tasks such as writing, research and presentation.
