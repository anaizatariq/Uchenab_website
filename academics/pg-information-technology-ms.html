Program Overview

Information Technology (MS)

The Masters in IT is an intensive, research-oriented degree that aims to grasp the intricacy of modern IT systems and the emerging need of protecting our IT systems in all organizations mainly academia and research development.

As a full-time, two-year graduate program, MSIT is designed to equip students with advanced IT skills necessary for implementing information security and assurance strategies. It is specifically designed to provide you with a solid foundation in learning the technical aspects of information security systems.

The basic intention of an academic program in Information Technology is to make cyberspace safe, secure, and reliable from all kinds of threats. You will learn to put your knowledge into practice by understanding system security threats and examining the ways of developing effective countermeasures to manage risks for information security breaches.

You will develop more focused and detailed knowledge of areas such as software, web technology, information management, networking, security, databases, and programming languages. Our graduates are trained to generate scientifically-grounded solutions on technology-related problems of information infrastructure, at both national and international community levels.

Modules

The proposed modules are assured to build a unified breadth and deep rooted curriculum just to keep in mind that similar aspects of potential areas in IT are covered completely. Moreover, graduate programs are based on prevailing foundation of fundamental components and vast range of choices of coursework.

Some additional important huge aspects could be added to the specialization list according to the resources available to the university. A student also have a choice to select either an MS thesis 6 credit hours or a 3 credit hour course and separate 3 credit hour MS project.

Teaching

In MSIT we use various methods of teachings through classroom activities including a combination of lectures, tutorials, lab sessions, seminars, workshops, online learning, and independent study.

This program structure is comprised of a total number of thirty to thirty-two lectures and about 2-3 hours per week will be allocated for teaching. You will be guided al about the studies, mainly about time for preparing classes, completion of research projects, and reading of primary texts. This program is taught in an appropriately positive learning environment for quality composing work to take place.

Assessment

Mainly evaluation will be concluded by extensive monitoring of written examination and course work completion along with routine quizzes, individual assignments, and group tasks such as writing, research and presentation.

Duration

The proposed degree must be completed within four-semester time. The least allocated duration for completion of MS degree program is about 2 years. And the maximum duration for completion on MS degree program is 4 years allocated by HEC.

Entry Requirement

Our entry requirements indicate both Standard and Minimum qualifications with which we normally accept students.

  1. 16 Years of Schooling / Equivalent in relevant field of study with at least 60% Marks / 2.5/4.00 CGPA from HEC Recognized Institution.
  2. Graduate Admission Test Administrated by the University.

Careers

With a master’s degree in IT, you will have a range of options open to you:

  • Computer and Information research scientist
  • Database Administrator
  • Information Security Analyst
  • Information Technology Analyst
  • Web Designer
  • User Experience Researcher
  • Network Administrator
  • System Analyst
  • Software Developer Analyst
  • Academic/ Researcher
  • Information System Auditor
  • Integration Specialist

Helping you find the right career

We have the facilities of devoted career counselors who help you to select your career choices and assist you in preparing for various recruitment programs. You can also be a part of career fest programs which are an interactive session with the recruiters so, you can understand their requirements and recruitment criteria.
For clearing any ambiguities visit Office of student affairs, The University of Chenab, Gujrat.

Tuition Fee

Mainly tuition fees majorly covers our study cost, which includes assessment and teaching. Advanced fees are taken before the academic semester. If the university takes fees directly then you can choose the installment methods to pay your fees.

Additional course costs

You may be charged an extra cost of additional things such as books, stationery, recreational and study trips, and other materials.

Funding

We offer several fully and partially funded postgraduate scholarships to full-time postgraduate students. Many external organizations aid in this funding programs which increases the amount of scholarships. For more information about funding visit office of student affairs.
