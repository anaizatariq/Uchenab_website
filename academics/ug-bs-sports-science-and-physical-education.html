Program Overview

The Bachelor of Science in Sports Science and Physical Education is a four-year undergraduate program designed to provide comprehensive knowledge and skills in sports performance, fitness training, physical education, and health sciences. The curriculum addresses the growing demand for professionals who can promote physical fitness, manage sports activities, and contribute to the development of athletic performance. Students develop expertise in exercise physiology, sports psychology, biomechanics, coaching science, and nutrition.

The program emphasizes key areas such as Human Anatomy, Physiology, Kinesiology, Biomechanics, Exercise Physiology, Sports Psychology, Health and Fitness Management, Sports Nutrition, Injury Prevention, Rehabilitation, and Coaching Methodologies. Students gain the scientific, practical, and leadership skills required to work as fitness experts, athletic trainers, coaches, and sports educators. The University of Chenab offers state-of-the-art training facilities, fully equipped fitness labs, and professional sports grounds to ensure practical, real-world experience essential for future careers.

Modules

Sports Science and Physical Education play a vital role in enhancing physical performance, promoting health, and preventing lifestyle diseases. This honors degree develops both theoretical knowledge and hands-on expertise essential for careers in fitness, sports coaching, physical education, and athletic development. Graduates acquire a strong foundation in human movement sciences, exercise prescription, injury management, and sports leadership.

The program provides students with a deep understanding of anatomy, physiology, biomechanics, sports psychology, coaching principles, and sports nutrition. Through interactive learning, students build effective communication, teamwork, leadership, and problem-solving skills. Research methods and evidence-based practices further strengthen their scientific approach to sports science.

Students gain practical experience in fitness assessments, exercise programming, strength and conditioning, injury rehabilitation, and performance evaluation. Innovative teaching techniques such as interactive case studies, lab-based simulations, and real-life coaching scenarios enhance learning outcomes. In the final year, students participate in supervised internships, clinical placements, and practical coaching assignments to refine their skills and professional competencies.

Eligibility Criteria

12 years of education/HSSC/A-Level/Intermediate (Pre-Medical) or equivalent with minimum 50% marks. UCHENAB Entry Test is mandatory.

Teaching

  1. General Principles

The BS Sports Science and Physical Education program is based on a combination of theoretical study and practical application. Students are encouraged to engage in self-directed learning, clinical exposure, research activities, and continuous academic development. Structured teaching sessions serve to complement this learning approach.

  1. Teaching Sessions

Formal teaching sessions are held regularly on working days, alongside practical training and fieldwork. These include seminars, coaching clinics, laboratory sessions, clinical demonstrations, interactive group discussions, research projects, and guest lectures by professionals from the sports industry.

  1. Teaching Schedule

The proposed departmental teaching schedule includes:

  • Delivering lectures: Daily
  • Presentations: Twice a month
  • Quiz-based activities: Twice a month
  • Research work/Practicals: Once a week
  • Interdepartmental meetings: Once a month
  • Guest lectures: Conducted from time to time
  • Library-based assignments: Conducted from time to time

Note:

  • All sessions are supervised by qualified faculty.
  • Each teaching activity is evaluated and graded by designated instructors.
  • Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance.
  • The Quality Assurance Department will monitor academic quality across all program components.
  • Evaluation tools will ensure ongoing improvements in teaching effectiveness.

Assessment

The assessment structure includes both theoretical examinations and practical evaluations for each subject. Students must demonstrate proficiency in both areas to advance through the program. A minimum of 50% marks is required in theory and practical exams independently.

Careers

Graduates of Sports Science and Physical Education enjoy diverse career opportunities across various sectors. Career prospects include:

  • Fitness training centers
  • Public sector sports institutions
  • Private fitness clubs and gyms
  • Schools and colleges as physical education instructors
  • Sports coaching centers
  • Athletic performance centers
  • Rehabilitation clinics
  • Health and wellness consulting
  • Sports event management companies
  • Research and academic journals
  • International career opportunities in the Middle East, Europe, USA, Canada, and beyond

The program ensures measurable outcomes by producing highly skilled professionals who meet the growing global demand for sports and fitness experts.

Helping you find the right career

The University of Chenab offers comprehensive career support services. Dedicated career counselors assist students in career planning, skill development, and job placement preparation. Students also benefit from specialized career fairs, networking events, and direct interaction with recruiters in the sports and fitness industry through the Office of Student Affairs.
