Program Overview

The Higher National qualifications in Business at the University of Chenab are designed for students who want to further their education through applied learning. Our HND Business program, in partnership with Pearson, offers a comprehensive study of the business sector, catering to those who aim to pursue or advance their careers in business. The curriculum provides essential knowledge, understanding, and skills foundational to the business sector and offers students a rich experience of its breadth and depth.

This program equips students for further study or training in various business careers, including management, administration, human resources, marketing, entrepreneurship, accounting, and finance.

  • Internationally Recognized Qualification: Offered by Pearson Edexcel, London, UK.
  • Global Reach: Pearson Edexcel operates in 112 countries, with over four million students annually pursuing its internationally recognized qualifications.
  • Equivalent to Two-Year BA/BSc in Pakistan: Providing a solid foundation for further academic pursuits.
  • Affordable and Economical: Accessible education without compromising on quality.
  • Direct Employment Opportunities: In-depth professional education highly regarded by employers.
  • Pathway to Further Education: Successful completion of HND allows direct entry to a one-year top-up to a BA/BSc Honors Degree at UK universities.
  • Early Professional Training: Ideal for students looking to start their professional journey early.
  • Assignment-Based Assessment: UCI supports students in completing their HND on time.

Careers

In collaboration with Pearson, the HND Business program at the University of Chenab equips you with a range of transferable skills to understand an organization at all levels. This comprehensive program covers various topics, allowing you to discover where you fit best within the business ecosystem. Upon graduating, you will be well-prepared to choose your desired career path. While studying, you will:

  • Gain an in-depth understanding of all business functions
  • Develop an analytical eye for important details and sharp critical thinking abilities
  • Become an expert problem solver and a confident communicator in business scenarios
  • Understand all levels of organizational structure and evaluate their positives and negatives through real-life case studies
  • Identify which aspects of business you enjoy most and begin to shape your career path

Our graduates have successfully ventured into various professions, including:

  • Brand Manager
  • Business Owner (Entrepreneur)
  • Business Developer
  • Digital Marketing Specialist
  • Project Manager
