Program Overview

Program

The Civil Engineering Technology (BSc) is an undergraduate degree program offered at The University of Chenab, Gujrat. It aims to develop skills that lead towards experiential learning of the fundamental principles of design, analysis, construction, installation, safety, sustainability, operation, and maintenance of buildings and other infrastructure systems essential to our modern society.

Duration

It is a full-time, four years degree giving you the chance to make active use of computer software and other innovative digital technologies like 3D printing, smart structures, Building Information Modeling Software, and SCIA Engineering to move construction to 5D or 7D dimensions.

Emphasis

With its dual emphasis on science and engineering, this course will enable our students to become acquainted with building engineering systems, structures, and technologies needed in the dynamic field of construction, infrastructure, and project management. This will enhance the quality of your professional skills required in team working, decision making, problem-solving, and engineering economics.

Opportunities

As a civil technologist, you can expect a variety of job opportunities in engineering design and development, structural engineering, geotechnical engineering, water resources engineering, site development, construction management, and the areas of environmental regulation.

Modules

Our Civil Engineering Technology modules are indicative of a wide range of courses. These modules provides you an opportunity to encounter a vast range of various engineering programs before your specialization. The program structure offers a flexible mixture of core/compulsory and optional modules.

Optional Module

Through the optional module, you have a choice to spend an extra year in a research exchange program, which will help you throughout your career.

Teaching

We use various methods of teachings through classroom activities including a combination of lectures, tutorials, lab sessions, seminars, workshops, online learning, and independent study.
Along with it, there will be many field trips to construction sites, factories, railways, and other projects of infrastructure building.

Number of lectures

This program structure is comprised of a total number of thirty to thirty-two lectures and about 2-3 hours per week will be allocated for teaching that includes 58% practical and 42% theory. You will be guided al about the studies, mainly about time for preparing classes, completion of research projects, and reading of primary texts.

Assessment

Mainly evaluation will be concluded by extensive monitoring of written examination and course work completion along with routine quizzes, individual assignments, and group tasks such as writing, research and presentation.

Careers

Completing a Bachelor’s degree in Civil Engineering Technology from the University of Chenab can open up several career opportunities for you both in Pakistan and abroad. Civil engineering technology is a field that affects our infrastructure and physical status in various aspects. The main objective of a civil technologist is to plan, construct, and maintain the infrastructure in the best physical shape in our society. These infrastructures include railways, tunnels, airports, highways, skyscrapers, and various mass transit projects. It is among the most established and opportunity-generating technology disciplines.

Major employers

Some major employers in the field of civil engineering technology in Pakistan are:

National Engineering Services Pakistan (state-owned enterprise)
Housing Urban Development & Public Health Engineering Department, Govt. of Punjab, Pakistan
Local Government and Community Development, Govt. of Punjab Pakistan
Planning and Development, Govt. of Punjab Pakistan
National Disaster Management Authority, Govt. of Pakistan
Pakistan Environmental Protection Agency, Govt. of Pakistan
Pakistan Horticulture Development and Export Company
Other Federal and Provincial Government agencies
Private Institutions

Other duties

In addition to working as an engineer, you may opt for other duties such as:

Statisticians, economists, and actuaries
Business and related professionals
Assessors, estimators, and values
Advisors, investment analysts, and finance
Manufacturing directors and production managers
Moreover, there are several career opportunities abroad as well, some of which are:

Construction Manager

As a construction manager, you can work on various construction projects such as buildings, bridges, and roads. Your responsibilities may include overseeing the entire construction process, ensuring that the project is completed on time and within budget.

Structural Engineer

As a structural engineer, you will be responsible for designing and analyzing various types of structures such as buildings, bridges, and tunnels. Your main aim will be to ensure that the structures are stable, durable, and safe.

Urban Planner

As an urban planner, you will be responsible for designing and managing various urban areas, such as neighborhoods, parks, and city centers. Your role will be to ensure that the areas are functional, aesthetically pleasing, and meet the needs of the community.

Geotechnical Engineer

As a geotechnical engineer, you will be responsible for designing and analyzing structures based on the soil and rock conditions in a specific location. Your main aim will be to ensure that the structures are stable and safe.

Transportation Engineer

As a transportation engineer, you will be responsible for designing and managing transportation systems such as highways, railways, and airports. Your role will be to ensure that the systems are efficient, safe, and meet the needs of the community.

Helping you find the right career

We have the facilities of devoted career counselors who help you to select your career choices and assist you in preparing for various recruitment programs. You can also be a part of career fest programs which are an interactive session with the recruiters so, you can understand their requirements and recruitment criteria.
