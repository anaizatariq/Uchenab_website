Faculty of Computer Science & Information Technology

The Faculty of Computer Science & Information Technology prepares students for careers in software, data, and artificial intelligence through an industry-aligned curriculum, modern labs, and close mentorship.

Programs offered

BS Computer Science, BS Software Engineering, BS Data Science, BS AI, MS Computer Science, MS Software Engineering, ADP Computing.

What we offer

Modern classrooms and laboratories, faculty who combine academic rigour with industry experience, opportunities to work on funded research and real client projects, and structured career support that begins in the first semester.

Ready to apply?

Applications for Fall 2026 are open. Apply online or explore fee structure and scholarships.
