Faculty Overview

Social Sciences Overview

The Faculty of Social Sciences at the University of Chenab is a dynamic and diverse community of scholars, researchers, and students. They are loyal to exploring the social, cultural, and economic dimensions of contemporary society. Our faculty members are experts in their fields. They hence bring a wealth of knowledge and experience to the classroom, as well as to our research and outreach activities.

Our faculty offers a range of undergraduate and graduate programs. This provide students with a deep understanding of the social sciences. They hence equip them with the skills and knowledge needed to succeed in their chosen careers. Our programs cover a wide variety of disciplines. This is including anthropology, sociology, psychology, economics, and political science, among others.

Main Goal

In addition to our academic programs, the Faculty of Social Sciences is also actively part of research and outreach activities. Our faculty members are engaged in cutting-edge research on a range of issues. Hence, they highlight the issues of social policy, economic development, globalization, and cultural diversity. Our research is aiming at generating new knowledge, and addressing the most pressing social, economic, and cultural challenges of our time.

Overall, the Faculty of Social Sciences at the University of Chenab is a vibrant and intellectually stimulating community of scholars, students, and practitioners. They are all contributing in advancing knowledge and making a positive impact on society.

Departments

Department of Languages Overview

Overview

The Department of English Language and Literature has already laid its basis since 2014 under the direction of The University of Lahore. The University of Chenab is carrying forward the zest and zeal with its legacy of the English Department. You will undergo an outstanding experience of education, one that strikes an ideal balance between language and literature. With a wide array of academic programs, we brace up our undergraduate and postgraduate students to hammer out careers in professional and academic job markets. Our graduates are highly qualified with credentials in drafted as well as vocal communication, analytical thinking, and problem solving. This enables them to fraternize and excel in increasingly kaleidoscopic and globalized world order. Their employability in fields are including customer service teaching, content creators, media, communication, civil service, and many others.

Scope of Degree

In the Department of Languages, the concerned faculty is responsible for awarding a 4-year program of BS English. Given the scope of this degree program, the instruction medium is English. Hence, different types of literature styles and literature periods of English are part of this program. It is understood with the help of different courses regarding to criticism and textual analysis. Meanwhile, both the comparative and American literature are thoroughly available to students. In this way, students can get a better understanding of various literatures and cultures. This program offers a vast prospect in which psychological, social, as well as biological viewpoint of the language. Additionally, elective courses are also available from various disciplines. Thus, the students are available with the information, skills, and competencies necessary to conduct interdisciplinary academic research.

We also target to foster an aptitude for modern learning and research among our students. For this, our faculty members dedicate themselves to impart collaborative and meticulous ambiance. Faculty areas of specialization are disparate and wide-ranging including studies in Applied Linguistics and Literature. MPhil English Literature and MPhil Applied Linguistics ensure students’ engagement with Reading and writing in various rhetorical contexts, interpreting and analysing a broad array of texts and genres, developing and sustaining criticality, understanding various approaches to second language acquisition, Multiple historical and cultural contexts of language and literature, the terminology, methodology and theories of English Studies, and majorly focusing on the production of independent research.

Future Outlook

In the next five years, we look forward to better integrating the department’s various programs by spotlighting 21st-Century Literary and Linguistic Studies. Our vision of literary studies centres on diversity, as indicated in our search priorities. Our classes and co-curricular programming will encourage students to see the difference the social sciences make in the various communities they engage and transit. We encourage faculty to join learning communities devoted to enhancing our pedagogy and making it more inclusive, we will further expand the pedagogical training and mentoring we offer to our graduate students.
