Program Overview

HND Health & Social Care

The Higher National Diploma (HND) in Health and Social Care at the University of Chenab provide a comprehensive understanding of the healthcare industry, addressing both the practical and ethical aspects of healthcare delivery. This program is essential for those looking to make a meaningful impact in the field of health and social care.

( HND ) Program Highlights:

Addressing Healthcare Needs:

Our program equips students with the knowledge and skills needed to meet the diverse healthcare needs of individuals and communities.

Commitment to Quality Care:

We emphasize the importance of providing high-quality care with compassion, ethics, and professionalism. Students are trained to ensure that healthcare services are delivered effectively and ethically.

Health Promotion and Prevention:

The curriculum includes vital coursework on health promotion and disease prevention, equipping students to encourage healthy behaviors and reduce the burden on healthcare systems.

Patient Advocacy:

Our graduates become strong advocates for patients’ rights, helping individuals navigate complex healthcare systems and access the care they need.

Meeting Demographic Challenges:

As populations age and face new health challenges, our program prepares students to provide effective and appropriate care to meet these evolving needs.

Community Health Focus:

The program emphasizes community-based healthcare approaches, teaching students how to collaborate with local communities to address specific health and social issues, promoting health equity.

Research and Innovation:

Students are encouraged to contribute to healthcare advancements through research and innovation, identifying new solutions, technologies, and policies to improve patient outcomes.

Ethical Decision-Making:

Our program includes comprehensive coursework on ethics, preparing students to make informed and ethically sound decisions in complex healthcare scenarios.

Diverse Career Opportunities:

Graduates have access to a wide range of career opportunities in healthcare management, public health, social work, counseling, and more. These careers are not only in demand but also highly fulfilling.

The Level 4 HNC and Level 5 HND in Health and Social Care at the University of Chenab are designed to prepare students for successful careers in this vital industry. Whether you’re looking to advance your current role or embark on a new career in health and social care, our program provides the skills and knowledge you need to succeed.

Modules

Year 1: Foundation and Core Skills

  1. Principles of Practice

  • This module introduces students to the key principles of care, including promoting equality, rights, and diversity within healthcare organizations. Students will learn about the value base of care and the roles and responsibilities of staff and managers in delivering a value-based service.

  1. Communication in Health and Social Care

  • Students will explore various forms of communication used in Health and Social Care settings, with a focus on IT skills. The module also covers the legal frameworks surrounding information recording and the use of technology as a communication tool.

  1. Health and Safety in Health and Social Care

  • This module covers current legislation related to health and safety, with a focus on risk assessment in care provision. Students will learn to identify high-risk and low-risk situations and understand the regulation and management of the social care workforce.

  1. Continuing Development in Health and Social Care

  • Students will explore the concept of professionalism, including self-management, personal effectiveness, and reflective practice. This module emphasizes ongoing professional development and the importance of partnership working and supporting other workers.

Year 2: Advanced Topics and Specialization

  1. Multi-Disciplinary Working in Health and Social Care

  • This module focuses on collaboration across different sectors and disciplines. Students will study legislation, policy, and the concept of stakeholder analysis, gaining insights into how to adapt Health and Social Care services to meet individual needs and ensure best practices.

  1. Independent Study in Health and Social Care

  • Students will develop their research and analytical skills by conducting an independent research project. This module emphasizes the use of appropriate research methods, including information technology and statistical analysis, to explore specific areas of health or social care.

  1. Quality and Service Improvement in Health and Social Care

  • This module teaches students how to enhance service quality through the development and review of standards. Students will learn about the principles of continuous improvement and the importance of organization-wide commitment to best practices.

  1. Ethical Considerations in Health and Social Care

  • Students will explore the ethical issues that arise in healthcare settings, learning to navigate complex ethical dilemmas and make informed decisions that impact care delivery and patient outcomes.

Duration

The minimum duration for completion of the HND – Art & Design is two years. 

Entry Requirement

At least 2 ‘A’ levels and 5 subjects of ‘O’ levels, Intermediate, Matric and Work-related experience. Appropriate English language skills

Careers

Graduates of the BTEC HND in Health and Social Care from the University of Chenab are well-equipped to pursue a variety of rewarding careers in the healthcare and social care sectors. The program’s comprehensive curriculum prepares students with the skills and knowledge needed to excel in various roles, including:

  • Healthcare Manager
  • Social Care Worker
  • Public Health Officer
  • Health Promotion Specialist
  • Patient Advocate
  • Care Home Manager
  • Counselor or Mental Health Support Worker
  • Health and Safety Officer
  • Rehabilitation Worker
