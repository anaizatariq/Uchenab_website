Program Overview

The pharmacy field is a cornerstone of healthcare, dedicated to innovating and enhancing pharmaceutical products to alleviate patient ailments. This dynamic and ever-evolving sector not only serves as a critical component of healthcare systems but also contributes significantly to the financial health of nations, given its vast and perpetual market demand.

The Department of Pharmacy at our university is committed to excellence in pharmaceutical education and offers the following programs:

  • Pharm-D (Doctor of Pharmacy): A comprehensive 5-year program.
  • MPhil Pharmaceutics: A specialized 2-year program.
  • MPhil Pharmacology: Another focused 2-year program.

These programs are designed to meet the rigorous standards set by the Pharmacy Council of Pakistan, ensuring our graduates are well-prepared for the demands of the field. Pharmacy, pharmaceutics, and pharmacology each have rich historical backgrounds and have evolved significantly over time. Today, pharmacists are integral to various healthcare settings, including:

  • Clinical pharmaceutical settings in hospitals and communities.
  • Pharmaceutical manufacturing.
  • Drug administrative organizations.
  • Research and development (discovery, manufacture, and delivery of drugs).
  • Training and coaching of healthcare staff.

Our Pharmaceutical Sciences unit is equipped with:

  • Upgraded lecture halls.
  • Conference and meeting rooms with high-end audio-visual setups.
  • State-of-the-art research laboratories with advanced supplies.

We emphasize practical experience through internship programs and research projects, allowing students to apply their learning in real-world settings. Our department ensures students receive rigorous training to work with precision in the field. Students are trained to:

  • Acquire ethical and technical skills.
  • Distinguish between technical and non-technical credentials in a regulated environment.
  • Develop strong decision-making abilities.
  • Work efficiently in crises.
  • Cultivate leadership qualities and problem-solving skills.
  • Handle administrative tasks effectively.
  • Ensure proper communication among healthcare departments, the community, and patients.

Teamwork is a hallmark of our institution. We prepare students to work collaboratively, ensuring seamless communication and cooperation between all components of the healthcare system.

Modules

Our institution offers students a variety of flexible options, allowing them to explore and shift between numerous courses throughout their five-year study duration. To build a solid foundation, the first two years are dedicated to a comprehensive core module.

Years 1 and 2: Focus on a vast core module to establish a strong groundwork in pharmaceutical sciences.

Year 3 Onward: Students apply their foundational knowledge to select specialized courses tailored to their interests and career goals.

By the third year, students will have a clear understanding of the significance of pharmacy in:

  • Solving medicinal problems.
  • Analyzing scientific content.
  • Delving into case histories.
  • Utilizing polymers to enhance drug delivery systems.

Teaching

1. Generally accepted Principles

Acquirement of factual and theoretical efficiency is the cornerstone for a graduate Doctor of Pharmacy. Learning on the graduate level is self-reliant process and essentially comprises on the knowledge acquired from studying research, performing practical tasks and from academic course work. The classes ensures that the core tasks are complemented by guidance from the teachers.

2. Coaching Sessions

All the coaching sessions and classes are conducted in the week days and no weekend classes are allowed on the weekends. Apart from this, team discussions, symposiums, demonstrations, quizzes, class activities, experimentation, outdoor hospital visits, research work and interdisciplinary meetings are also conducted.

3. Teaching Plan

The advised teaching plan is mentioned below:

1. Delivering lectures. Daily

4. Presentation. Twice a month

3. Quiz based activities Twice a month

5. Research work

Practical’s Once a week

Once in a month

From time to time

from time to time
Point to be noted:

  • The quality assurance department will monitor the program components as per the scheme of the study.

Research activities

Assessment

All the graduate students will be examined on a daily basis through academic activities. This assessment will continue periodically as well.

  1. General Principles
  2. The examination is assessment is conclusive, fair, and dependable.
  3. Developmental, continuous, and final examination is help for theory as well a practical work. Besides, assessment of research is a separate task.
  4. Formative Assessment
    This examination is both continuous and end-of-term. The continuous one is for the theoretical course work as well as experimental skills. The latter in the end of each semester. The former is based on the theoretical and practical work concerned. End of term (semester exam) assessment is held at the end of each semester (up to the 10th semester).
    This will ensure proper feedback to the students as well as to the faculty members to solve any academic problems/issues for further improvement.
    3.Internal examination
    The accomplishment of students within and after the study period will be examined continuously and will be recorded as well. This record will be an evidence of students hard work and performance throughout the semester and marks will be awarded on the basis of this record.
  5. Summative Assessment
    The division of the marks will be according to the subject and include theory and practical performance. Passing criteria is 50%. It will be compulsory for the student to pass both the practical as well as theory separately.
