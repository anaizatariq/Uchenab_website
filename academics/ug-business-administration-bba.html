Program Overview

The Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) program offered by the Department of Business Education at the University of Chenab is a four-year undergraduate degree program designed to provide students with the knowledge and skills necessary to succeed in today’s challenging business environment. The program covers a wide range of business and management areas, including finance, entrepreneurship, marketing, management, and human resources.

Knowledge

By studying BBA, students gain practical knowledge of how businesses operate, and they develop a solid background in business and management. The program aims to develop managerial leadership skills, effective communication strategies, job performance duties, new product development, mathematical skills, microfinance, social responsibility, sustainability, business planning, industry reporting, critical thinking, problem-solving, and ethical decision-making.

What we offer?

The BBA program offers a flexible mixture of core/compulsory and optional modules, covering all essential areas of business and management necessary for a successful career on a global scale. Real-life case studies are taught by leading academics who are well-connected with the business world, combining their study with work experience.

Options

Students also have the option to undertake a work placement with an external organization as an optional module, providing valuable experience to add to their CV. Alternatively, students can choose coursework instead of a placement year. Overall, the BBA program aims to prepare students for professional careers in various business fields and to become successful in today’s global business environment.

Modules

Year one 

Year One introduces key skills necessary for the advanced study of Business Administration. This module will introduce the fundamental concepts of business management. You will study Business, Economics, entrepreneurship, business creation and growth, principles of Markets, Marketing and Selling, Accounting, and Finance. Year One module will also provide the study of Analytical methods for Business, Language and Context of Business, and Basic Economic issues.

Year Two

The Year Two modules available will focus on Managing Innovation, International Business Strategies, Organizational Behavior and Management, Business Research Methods, Operations of supply chain management, professional and academic development.

Year three

Year three and four is a work placement module that will enable you to apply your skills in a real work setting. Other modules listed within year Three emphasize the study of Business strategy, Business Administration, computational thinking, Managerial Accounting, Business Law, and Operations Management.

Eligibility Criteria

The Bachelor of Business Administration program is of 4-years duration, spread over 8 regular semesters, and consisting of 135 credit hours after completing twelve years of higher secondary school certificate or equivalent. At least, 45% marks in intermediate or equivalent are required for admission in BBA.

Tuition Fee

Tuition fees cover the majority of the costs of your study, including teaching and assessment. Fees are charged at the start of each academic semester. If you pay your fees directly to the University, you can also choose to pay in instalments.

Additional course costs

There may be extra costs related to your course for things such as stationery, books, materials, and field trips.

Funding

We offer several fully and partially funded undergraduate scholarships to full-time undergraduate students. Visit Funding Policy.

Teaching

Academic modules are taught through a combination of lectures, tutorials, computer-based lab sessions, seminars, workshops, online learning, and independent study. The number of modules studied varies per year and is the equivalent of a weekly two-three-hour lecture. You will be guided about study time preparing for classes, reading primary texts, and completing research projects.
Our Bachelor’s degree in Business

Skills

Administration ensures strong skill-based business learning in a highly conducive and productive environment. Auxiliary online materials are available and there will be various events and activities to further enhance the learning.

Assessment

All the modules are assessed by coursework, including individual assignments, real-business case studies, interview projects, online quizzes, group work, and poster presentations.

Careers

Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) degree holders are eligible for pursuing their Postgraduate degrees in the subject of Business Administration and thus become further capable for the job of:

  • Professional Business Analyst
  • Business Consultant
  • Assistant Marketing Director
  • Office Manager
  • Computing Business coordinator
  • Industrial salesperson
  • Appraiser and assessor of real estate
  • Accounts examiner
  • payroll assistant

Our Business Administration (BBA) degree will enable you to start in any business either as an entrepreneur to build your firm from the ground up or to work for a large, global corporation and administer the project successfully.

Helping you find the right career

We have a dedicated careers counselor who can support you with your career choices and skills to prepare you for recruitment processes. You can also attend tailored careers and networking events to connect with recruiters in your field.
