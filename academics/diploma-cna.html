Program Overview

The Certified Nursing Assistant (CNA) Diploma Program at the University of Chenab provides a rigorous two-year education. It is available for aspiring nursing professionals. Accredited by the Pakistan Nursing Council, this program hence ensures high standards of quality and relevance in nursing education.

Program Highlights:

    • Accredited Excellence: Our program is official by the Pakistan Nursing Council. It is guaranteeing a curriculum that meets national standards as well as prepares students for professional success.
    • Comprehensive Curriculum: Offers a well-structure form of education covering essential nursing knowledge. It highlights practical skills, as well as professional attitudes required for a thriving career in healthcare.
    • Hands-On Experience: It includes extensive clinical training through placements as well as simulation labs. This is hence allowing students to apply their learning in real-world healthcare settings.
    • Professional Development: It focuses on cultivating the professional behaviors and ethical standards. Thus, this is necessary for providing compassionate and competent patient care.

    The Student who will complete the CNA Diploma Program at the University of Chenab are equipped with the expertise and credentials to excel as Certified Nursing Assistants, contributing positively to patient care and advancing their careers in the healthcare industry.

It hence empowers students with the knowledge, skills, as well as professional attitudes. Thus, this is necessary for excellence in nursing care. We are dedicated to:

    • Delivering High-Quality Education: It is offering an integrated curriculum that combines theoretical knowledge with practical experience. Thus, it helps to prepare students for effective patient care.

    • Supporting Career Development: It is hence equipping students with the tools and confidence to pursue successful careers as Certified Nursing Assistants. Meanwhile, it is contributing positively to patient care and the healthcare community.

    Our program is aiming in developing skilful and keen nursing professionals who will excel in their roles and make a significant impact on the quality of patient care.

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification: Applicant must meet one of the following academic levels
FSc (Pre-Medical) with 45% marks minimum (Physics, Chemistry & Biology as compulsory subjects)
Matric (Science) with 45 % marks minimum (Physics, Chemistry & Biology as compulsory subjects)
Matric (Arts) with 50% marks, FA with 50% Marks, UCHENAB Entry Test is mandatory.
Age Limit: 14-35 years

Careers

Graduates of the Certified Nursing Assistant (CNA) Diploma Program at the University of Chenab have a wide range of career opportunities in the healthcare field. Equipped with comprehensive training as well as practical experience, they can pursue roles such as:

Certified Nursing Assistant (CNA)

Provide direct patient care by assisting with daily activities, monitoring vital signs, as well as supporting overall patient well-being. It plays a key role in hospitals, nursing homes, and other healthcare settings.

Geriatric Care Aide

Specialize in elderly care, offering tailored support and assistance to seniors in long-term care facilities as well as in-home settings.

Home Health Aide

Deliver personalized care in patients’ homes, helping with activities of daily living and health monitoring. It is hence fostering a supportive environment.

Hospice Aide

Offer compassionate care to terminally ill patients and their families, providing comfort as well as support during end-of-life care.

Rehabilitation Aide

It hence assists patients in physical therapy settings, supporting their recovery through exercises and mobility assistance.

Medical Assistant

Support healthcare professionals with clinical tasks such as taking vital signs, preparing patients for exams, and managing administrative responsibilities.

Patient Care Technician

Perform essential healthcare tasks, including monitoring vital signs, assisting with medical procedures, as well as delivering direct patient care.

Healthcare Support Worker

Work in various healthcare environments. It is hence providing vital support to patients and ensuring their needs are met effectively.

Emergency Medical Technician (EMT)

Utilize CNA training to deliver emergency medical care and transport patients to medical facilities in critical situations.

Clinical Care Coordinator

Oversee and coordinate patient care plans, ensuring seamless integration of services and effective treatment delivery across different healthcare providers.

Meanwhile, these career paths reflect the versatility and demand for skilled CNAs, offering rewarding opportunities to make a significant impact in the healthcare sector.
