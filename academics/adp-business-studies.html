Program Overview

Comprehensive two-year program for foundational business knowledge and practical skills, for quick workforce entry or further study in business disciplines.

Entrepreneurship

Develop the mindset and skills to start and run your own business.

Interactive Learning

Lectures, computer-based lab sessions, seminars, workshops, and online learning.

Real-Business Cases

Understand consumer behavior and market dynamics via industry case studies.

Admission & Fees

Eligibility Criteria

2 years/4 semesters, 69 credit hours after 12 years schooling or equivalent. Minimum 40% marks in intermediate or equivalent.

Tuition Fee

Total incl. Rs 20,000 registration: Rs 334,300.

Teaching & Assessment

Teaching Methodology

Lectures, tutorials, computer-based lab sessions, seminars, workshops, online learning, independent study, industry events/guest lectures.

Assessment Methods

Individual assignments, real-business case studies, interview projects, online quizzes, group work, poster presentations.

Career Pathways

Business Analyst, Marketing Coordinator, Financial Assistant, HR Officer, Sales Executive, Customer Service Rep, Entrepreneur, Retail Manager, Banking Sector roles.

Further education and certification, or stepping directly into the 5th semester of a BS program, can significantly enhance career prospects and open doors to global opportunities.

HR Officer

Manage talent, recruitments, and organizational culture.

Marketing Coordinator

Drive brand awareness, public relations, and product campaigns.

Entrepreneur

Launch and scale your own business ventures or retail outlets.

Business Analyst

Optimize operations, financial planning, and business strategies.
