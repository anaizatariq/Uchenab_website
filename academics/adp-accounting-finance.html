Program Overview

Our Associate Degree Program (ADP) in Accounting and Finance is meticulously designed to provide students with a strong foundational knowledge of accounting and finance principles. This program integrates practical and theoretical aspects of business management, ensuring students are well-prepared for immediate entry into the workforce.

Interactive Learning

Engage in lectures, seminars, and group discussions led by experienced faculty.

Real-World Skills

Apply knowledge through real-life case studies, simulations, and projects.

Professional Development

Gain essential soft skills like leadership, communication, and teamwork.

Internships

Benefit from internships with top firms to gain practical experience.

Specialization Options

Tailor your studies with a focus on accounting, finance, or both.

Admission & Fees

Eligibility Criteria

2 years/4 semesters, 69 credit hours after 12 years of schooling or equivalent. Minimum 40% marks in intermediate or equivalent.

Tuition Fee

Sem 1: Rs 75,300 | Sem 2: Rs 72,400 | Sem 3: Rs 83,800 | Sem 4: Rs 82,800 — Total incl. Rs 20,000 registration: Rs 334,300

Teaching & Assessment

Teaching Methodology

Interactive learning (lectures/seminars), real-world case studies, professional development (soft skills), internships, specialization options (accounting/finance/both), academic advisor support.

Assessment Methods

Individual assignments, case study analysis, projects, online quizzes, group work, presentations.

Career Pathways

Accountant, Financial Analyst, Auditor, Tax Consultant, Financial Manager, Entrepreneur.

Further education and certification, or stepping directly into the 5th semester of a BS program, can significantly enhance career prospects and open doors to global opportunities.

Accountant

Prepare financial statements, manage budgets, and ensure compliance.

Financial Analyst

Analyze financial data, prepare models, and provide investment recommendations.

Auditor

Review financial records, identify risks, and recommend improvements.

Tax Consultant

Provide advice on tax planning, prepare tax returns, and assist with audits.

Financial Manager

Oversee financial operations, manage budgets, and make strategic decisions.

Entrepreneur

Start businesses, leveraging accounting and finance skills to manage finances.
