The University of Chenab recognizes that sports are not only about physical fitness but also essential for building leadership, teamwork, and discipline among students. We understand the importance of balancing academics with extracurricular activities to promote holistic growth. That’s why we actively encourage our students to participate in a wide range of sports, providing them with opportunities to develop both on and off the field.

To support this, we offer

Inter-departmental Matches

Regular matches between departments to foster healthy competition and unity.

Exceptional Sports Facilities

A fully equipped sports area with modern amenities.

Sports Equipment

High-quality gear for various sports, ensuring the best experience for athletes.

The University of Chenab hosts tournaments in sports like badminton, futsal, and cricket. Our students proudly represent UChenab in national tournaments across Pakistan, forming teams that embody the university’s spirit of excellence and teamwork.

In addition, we offer e-sports competitions, providing students with opportunities to compete in digital sports alongside traditional athletics.
