Office of Administration

The Admissions Office is committed to managing the University's admission processes in a transparent, fair, and student-centered manner.

Aligned with the University's vision of academic growth and inclusivity, we strive to guide prospective students at every stage of the admission journey. Through clear communication, accurate information, and continuous support, we facilitate a smooth and welcoming experience, enabling students and stakeholders to engage with confidence and clarity.
