Answers to the questions we are asked most often by applicants and their families. If your question is not covered here, contact the Office of Admissions and we will be glad to help.

Applying

All applications are submitted online through the UChenab admission portal. Register with your email and mobile number, complete the form, upload your documents and pay the processing fee. See our How To Apply guide for the full process.

Admissions open twice a year for the Fall (September) and Spring (February) semesters. Advertisements are published in national newspapers, on the university website and on our official social media channels.

Yes. The application form allows you to list multiple programme preferences in order of priority.

Yes. You may apply on a provisional basis and submit your result card before the deadline announced by the Office of Admissions.

No. The processing fee is non-refundable and non-transferable.

Eligibility & entry tests

Undergraduate programmes generally require Intermediate or an equivalent qualification with at least 45–50% marks. Some programmes such as Pharm-D, DPT and Engineering Technology require pre-medical or pre-engineering subject combinations and higher marks. Graduate programmes require a relevant 16-year qualification.

Certain programmes require a departmental entry test and/or interview. You will be informed through the portal and by SMS if your programme requires one.

You must provide an equivalence certificate issued by the Inter Board Committee of Chairmen (IBCC) along with your foreign transcripts.

Yes, credit transfer and migration cases are considered on merit, subject to HEC rules and the approval of the relevant Board of Studies.

Fees, scholarships & financial assistance

Fees vary by programme. The complete programme-wise fee structure is available from the Office of Admissions and on the Fee Structure page.

Yes. An instalment facility is available for semester dues on application to the Finance Office.

UChenab offers merit-based scholarships, need-based financial assistance, kinship and orphan support, sports scholarships, scholarships for students with disabilities, inter-provincial scholarships, alumni scholarships and M.Phil./MS scholarships. See Scholarships & Aid.

No. Only one scholarship or concession applies at any given time; where a student qualifies for more than one, the most favourable is awarded.

Submit the prescribed financial assistance application form to the Financial Aid Office along with income evidence and supporting documents. Applications are reviewed by the scholarship committee each semester.

Campus life

Yes. The university operates point-to-point bus routes serving Gujrat, Gujranwala, Sialkot, Wazirabad, Kharian, Lalamusa, Jhelum, Mandi Bahauddin and surrounding areas.

Guidance on accommodation is provided by the Office of Student Affairs. Contact them for current availability and charges.

Modern laboratories, a well-stocked library, high-speed IT infrastructure, a cafeteria, sports grounds, prayer areas, a medical room and student societies.

On the G.T. Road adjacent to the Chenab Bridge, Gujrat — 26 km from Sialkot International Airport. See our Location page.
