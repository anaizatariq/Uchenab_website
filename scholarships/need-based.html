Need-Based Financial Assistance

Up to 50% of tuition fee for students facing genuine financial hardship.

UChenab provides need-based financial assistance of up to 50% of tuition fee to students facing genuine financial hardship. Assessment is conducted by the Institutional Scholarship Award Committee (ISAC) through a structured process including document verification and an interview.

There is no fixed entitlement — the assistance is determined case by case within the 50% ceiling.

How the decision is made

Interview of the student

A structured interview with the applicant, and where required with parents or guardians.

Financial position

Assessment of the household income and financial circumstances of the student, parents or guardians.

Validity of documents

Verification of income evidence, utility bills and the property statement submitted.

Home visit if needed

The committee may arrange a visit to verify the declared circumstances.

Rules

  • The financial assistance percentage is applied to tuition fees only.
  • The approved assistance is subject to the general rules and is reviewed under the financial assistance policy.
  • Granted assistance is cancelled under the conditions stated in point (vii) of the general rules, with no right of review.
  • MS/M.Phil. students may also apply for need-based assistance, which will not exceed 35% of the tuition fee.

Maintenance and reinstatement criteria

  • Students admitted under need-based categories must maintain a minimum SGPA/CGPA of 3.00 to continue receiving the full percentage awarded at admission.
  • If the SGPA/CGPA falls below 3.00 but remains at or above 2.75, the award is adjusted under the approved sliding-scale formula.
  • If the SGPA/CGPA falls below 2.75, the scholarship is discontinued.
  • Reinstatement in full may be requested on regaining a CGPA of 3.00 or above, with a maximum of two reinstatements during the programme.

Documents required

  • Completed Financial Assistance Form issued by the Office of Student Affairs.
  • Income evidence for the parents or guardians (salary slips, income certificate or affidavit).
  • Recent utility bills (electricity, gas and telephone).
  • Property statement.
  • Copies of CNIC of the applicant and the father or guardian.
  • Any additional documentary evidence requested by the committee.

How to apply

Applications for scholarships and financial assistance must be submitted to the Office of Student Affairs (OSA) within two weeks of the commencement of classes, or within two weeks of payment of the first fee instalment, whichever is earlier. Late applications are not ordinarily entertained.

For need-based assistance, applicants must submit financial documents including income evidence, utility bills and a property statement. An interview may be required.

The “Pay It Forward” Pledge

Every scholarship recipient at UChenab signs the Pay It Forward Pledge — a commitment to mentor or support at least one deserving student during their professional career.

To sustain this culture of giving back, UChenab is developing an Alumni Endowment Contribution Programme, through which scholarship alumni will be invited to voluntarily support future scholarships. The programme, scheduled for launch by December 2026, will recognise contributors through naming rights at defined contribution levels. An initial proposal encourages alumni to contribute a minimum of Rs. 5,000 annually to support students of their alma mater.

Apply for support

Applications go to the Office of Student Affairs within two weeks of the start of classes, or within two weeks of paying your first instalment — whichever is earlier.
