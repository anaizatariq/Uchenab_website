Merit scholarships acknowledge and encourage brilliant, top-performing students. They are awarded at admission on the basis of HSSC aggregate marks (or undergraduate CGPA for postgraduate programmes) and continue subject to CGPA maintenance each semester.

Effective from: Fall 2026. This policy does not cover students of post-graduate (Ph.D.) degree programmes.

Eligibility

  • BS degree programmes — awarded to students who have passed the Intermediate / HSSC examination, per the criteria below.
  • MS / M.Phil. degree programmes — awarded to students who have passed BS / Master / 16-year examinations, per the criteria below. Where the degree was completed through a semester system, a detailed marks certificate may be provided.
  • 5th semester induction programmes — awarded to students who have passed 14-year / ADP examinations.

Procedure

Apply at the admission counter

Submit the merit-based scholarship form along with your admission application and required documents.

Admission office review

After selection of the candidate, the admission office forwards the form and documents to the Fee Section.

Fee voucher issued

The Fee Section issues the fee voucher once it receives the completed forms and documents.

Awaited results

If your HSSC result is awaited, your documents are processed on the Part I result against an undertaking; the percentage is adjusted if the final result does not meet the criteria.

Award and notification

The scholarship is granted in the system after registration, and the Fee Section notifies the list of successful students.

Rules and regulations

  • Merit-based scholarships are granted after verification of submitted documents.
  • Apply within two weeks of the start of classes, or within two weeks of paying the first instalment.
  • Applications without the required documents will not be processed.
  • The scholarship is granted on tuition fees only.
  • Scholarships are available in regular Fall and Spring admissions only.
  • A student may hold only one scholarship at a time; the highest available percentage is awarded.
  • Any student, parent or guardian may apply to discontinue the scholarship through an application to OSA.

The university may cancel a merit scholarship for:

  • Disciplinary issues or misconduct
  • Rustication
  • Expulsion
  • De-registration
  • Fraudulent act

Maintenance and reinstatement criteria

  • Students admitted on a merit scholarship must maintain a minimum SGPA/CGPA of 3.50 to continue receiving the full percentage awarded at admission.
  • If the SGPA/CGPA falls below 3.50 but remains at or above 3.25, the award is adjusted in line with the approved sliding-scale formula.
  • If the SGPA/CGPA falls below 3.25, the scholarship is discontinued.
  • A student whose scholarship has been discontinued may apply for full reinstatement on regaining a CGPA of 3.50 or above. A maximum of two reinstatements is permitted during the programme.

How to apply

Applications for scholarships and financial assistance must be submitted to the Office of Student Affairs (OSA) within two weeks of the commencement of classes, or within two weeks of payment of the first fee instalment, whichever is earlier. Late applications are not ordinarily entertained.

For need-based assistance, applicants must submit financial documents including income evidence, utility bills and a property statement. An interview may be required.

The “Pay It Forward” Pledge

Every scholarship recipient at UChenab signs the Pay It Forward Pledge — a commitment to mentor or support at least one deserving student during their professional career.

To sustain this culture of giving back, UChenab is developing an Alumni Endowment Contribution Programme, through which scholarship alumni will be invited to voluntarily support future scholarships. The programme, scheduled for launch by December 2026, will recognise contributors through naming rights at defined contribution levels. An initial proposal encourages alumni to contribute a minimum of Rs. 5,000 annually to support students of their alma mater.

Apply for support

Applications go to the Office of Student Affairs within two weeks of the start of classes, or within two weeks of paying your first instalment — whichever is earlier.
